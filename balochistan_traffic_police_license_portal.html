Official Traffic Directives

Traffic Hadayat & Driving Guidelines

Essential safety regulations and traffic laws enforced by Balochistan Traffic Police across urban roads, highways, and mountain passes.

RULE 01
Mandatory Safety Gear

Seatbelt & Safety Helmet Rule

All car drivers and front passengers must wear seatbelts at all times. Motorcycle riders and pillion passengers are legally mandated to wear safety helmets approved by traffic authorities.

RULE 02
Speed Enforcement

Speed Limits & Speed Cam Zones

Adhere strictly to designated speed limits: 50 km/h within Quetta city zones, and 100 km/h on national highways (N-25 Quetta-Chaman / N-50 / Makran Coastal Highway).

RULE 03
Zero Tolerance

No Mobile Phone While Driving

Using handheld mobile phones, texting, or attending calls while steering a vehicle is strictly prohibited and carries heavy fines and immediate challan issuance.

RULE 04
Highways & Mountain Passes

Lane Discipline & Blind Turns

Do not overtake on sharp curves or double solid lines across Bolan Pass and Lakpass. Give way to uphill heavy transport vehicles on mountainous highway routes.

RULE 05
Vehicle Documentation

Original Plates & Fitness Proof

Fancy, tinted, or unauthorized license plates are strictly illegal. Carry your valid physical driving license, CNIC, and official vehicle registration book at all times.

RULE 06
Night & Winter Travel

High Beam & Fog Light Rules

Switch to low beams inside city limits to prevent blinding oncoming drivers. Maintain double fog lamps and extended distance during winter fog in Quetta and Kalat.
