Official Safety Rules

Traffic Hadayat & Driving Guidelines

Key road safety regulations enforced by Balochistan Traffic Police for safe driving on highways and city roads.

RULE 01
Mandatory Safety Gear

Seatbelt & Safety Helmet Mandate

Car drivers and front passengers must wear seatbelts at all times. Motorcycle riders and pillion passengers must wear safety helmets approved by traffic authorities.

RULE 02
Speed Enforcement

Speed Limits & Speed Cam Zones

Adhere strictly to designated speed limits: 50 km/h inside city limits, and 100 km/h on national highways (N-25 Quetta-Chaman / N-50 / Coastal Highway).

RULE 03
Zero Tolerance

No Mobile Use While Driving

Using handheld phones, texting, or attending calls while operating a vehicle is strictly prohibited and results in immediate challan issuance.

RULE 04
Mountain Passes

Lane Discipline on Mountain Curves

Never overtake on blind turns or double solid lines along Bolan Pass and Lakpass. Always yield right of way to heavy vehicles traveling uphill.

RULE 05
Documentation

Standard Plates & Valid Documents

Fancy or non-standard license plates are strictly prohibited. Always carry your valid physical driving license, CNIC, and vehicle registration.

RULE 06
Night & Winter Travel

High Beam & Fog Safety

Switch to low beams within city areas to avoid blinding oncoming traffic. Ensure functional fog lights during low-visibility winter weather.
